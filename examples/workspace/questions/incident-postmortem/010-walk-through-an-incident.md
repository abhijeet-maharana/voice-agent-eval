# Walk me through a production incident you handled, as a postmortem.

**Priority:** 1

## Short Answer
Timeline first: how it was detected, when, and by whom. Impact in numbers (users affected, minutes, failed requests). What you did to stop the damage (mitigation: rollback, feature flag, scaling up). The root cause, found after the system was stable. Follow-up actions with owners: the alert, test or runbook that stops it happening again. Blameless: about the system, not a person.

## Going Deeper
A strong answer keeps mitigation and fix apart, names how long detection took and why, explains how the team communicated during the incident (one channel, one incident lead, status updates), and includes a follow-up that improves detection, not only the bug fix. It says what the speaker personally did.
